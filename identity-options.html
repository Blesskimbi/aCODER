Motif — the aperture

The logo's nested triangles, redrawn as an open aperture. Used as: section rules, bento card backdrops, the scroll-tour progress ring, loading states, and the 3D hero object.

Applied

TOON compression
30–70% fewer tokens on structured tool results. Opt-in.
Why this one
  • It is already A-Coder's colour — the IDE's own accent, so site and product match by construction.
  • Cool teal is the correct partner for brushed steel; it reads as patina on the metal, not a sticker over it.
  • 195° is empty space — clear of freebuff's lime, Cursor's mono, Linear's indigo, Supabase's emerald.
  • The motif is our real mark, so it costs nothing to justify and scales from favicon to 3D hero.
Trade-off
  • Teal is the most common "developer tool" accent after blue — it needs the light weights and the 3D logo to feel singular.

Motif — the light

Not a shape but the raking light itself. A 112° sweep lights every section's top-left, borders brightening where it lands — literalising the logo's lit-metal edge.

Applied

Agent running
Multi-file edits with per-tool permissions.
Strengths
  • The most distinctive of the three — nobody in this category owns steel-plus-ember.
  • Derived from the logo's actual lighting, so the mark and the site share one physical logic.
  • Warm accent on cold metal is a genuinely premium, industrial combination.
Trade-off
  • It contradicts the product. The IDE's DESIGN.md specifies teal; an orange site would diverge from the app — the opposite of the brief's "one brand" requirement.
  • Orange is close to the existing --warning token, risking semantic confusion.
  • Requires either an IDE re-skin or accepting the mismatch.

Strengths
